<objective>
End-to-end tracer for the parent-vs-child required-level contradiction finding: prove one narrow YELLOW self-status path works from `BusinessCapabilityChain` shape → `evaluator` classification → `markers` projection → assertion. Scoped to the ROOT-vs-its-own-direct-parents case only (D-01's first half). Descendant-vs-parent inside the subtree recursion, multi-parent independent-edge iteration (D-02), and the Neo4j Cypher fetch that supplies `parentRequiredLevels` in production are deferred to Plan 02.3-02.

Purpose: prove the pipeline (types → evaluator → markers → finding surfaces as YELLOW self) works end-to-end on a hand-built fixture BEFORE we touch Cypher or expand recursion, so any architectural dead-end shows up immediately on the smallest possible surface area.

Output: a fixture-driven test where `analyzeBusinessCapability` on a root whose `parentRequiredLevels[i].required[dim]` is HIGHER than its own `chain.required[dim]` produces a `Finding` with `status='YELLOW'`, `violatingElementType='businessCapability'`, and `projectMarkers(analysis).get(rootId).selfStatus === 'YELLOW'`.
</objective>
