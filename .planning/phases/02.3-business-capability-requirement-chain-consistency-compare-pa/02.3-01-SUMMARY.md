# Phase 02.3 Plan 01: Parent-vs-Child Required-Level Contradiction Tracer Summary

**Adds `classifyCapabilityAgainstParent` and wires it into `analyzeBusinessCapability` so a business capability whose own required sovereignty level is weaker than its direct parent's requirement is flagged as a YELLOW, self-violating finding (D-01/D-06), while every other capability keeps its existing GREY-self invariant (D-05) — root-only scoping, descendant/multi-parent cases deferred to Plan 02.3-02.**

- **Files modified:** 7

## Accomplishments

- `types.ts`: `ViolatingElementType` extended with `'businessCapability'` (documented as the D-06 exception — a capability can be the violating element of a required-level *contradiction*, not just an achieved-value violation). `BusinessCapabilityChain` gained `readonly parentRequiredLevels: readonly { readonly id: string; readonly required: RequirementLevels }[]`, documented as root-only (repository populates it only for the analysis root; nested `childCapabilities` always carry `[]`).
- `evaluator.ts`: new exported `classifyCapabilityAgainstParent(chain, parentEntry, chainPathPrefix)` iterates all four sovereignty dimensions independently, skipping any dimension where either side's required level is `null`, and emits a `YELLOW` finding with `violatingElementType: 'businessCapability'` whenever the child's required maturity is lower than the parent's. `analyzeBusinessCapability` now combines the existing subtree findings with `chain.parentRequiredLevels.flatMap(...)` results before computing `downstreamStatus`.
- `markers.ts`: both the findings-loop and the backfill-loop now recognize a capability that is itself the `violatingElementId` of a `businessCapability`-typed finding and give it a real `selfStatus` (via `worseStatus`) instead of the usual hard-coded `GREY` — every other capability (no own-finding) keeps the pre-existing GREY-self invariant unchanged.
- `repository.ts`: added `parentRequiredLevels: []` to the existing `fetchBusinessCapabilityChain` node literal — a mechanical, non-Cypher change required purely to keep the codebase type-checking after the `types.ts` field became mandatory. The real Neo4j fetch of parent required levels is explicitly deferred to Plan 02.3-02.
- `__tests__/fixtures.ts`: `parentRequiredLevels: []` added to all 10 pre-existing `BusinessCapabilityChain` fixtures (including nested `childCapabilities` entries). Two new fixtures added: `rootParentContradictionFixture` (child `security: MEDIUM` vs. parent `security: HIGH`) and `rootParentNoContradictionFixture` (child `security: HIGH` vs. parent `security: MEDIUM`).
- `__tests__/evaluator.test.ts` / `__tests__/markers.test.ts`: Tests A–F added covering the single-dimension contradiction case, null-exclusion on either side, the no-contradiction case, the multi-dimension independent-findings case, and the marker-level YELLOW-self exception vs. the GREY-self invariant. All 6 pre-existing `evaluator.test.ts` tests and all 5 pre-existing `markers.test.ts` tests remain unmodified and pass.
