<objective>
Extend Phase 02.3's required-vs-required parent/child tree-consistency check so a `BusinessCapability`'s `selfStatus` (as projected by `projectMarkers`) becomes three-valued instead of two-valued: **GREEN** when its own required level was explicitly, meaningfully compared against at least one parent and found consistent; **YELLOW** on contradiction (02.3's existing behavior, unchanged); **GREY** only when there is genuinely nothing to compare (no parent at all, or every dimension excluded on every parent).

Task 1 is the tracer: thread a new "was a real comparison made" signal (`hasRealComparison` / `comparedCapabilityIds`) from `evaluator.ts` through to `markers.ts`, proven end-to-end on the root-only case (mirrors 02.3-01's root-only tracer precedent) using the already-existing `rootParentNoContradictionFixture`. Task 2 expands the proven mechanism to the descendant recursion and D-02 multi-parent composition, since both already ride the same `classifyCapabilityAgainstParent` call sites Task 1 rewires — no new traversal, no new Neo4j fetch (unlike 02.3-02, which needed one).

Purpose: today "nothing was ever compared" and "compared and passed" are structurally indistinguishable by the time data reaches `markers.ts` (both produce zero `Finding`s) — 03-RESEARCH.md confirms this cannot be derived from existing data and a new intermediate signal must be threaded through. Proving this on the smallest fixture (root vs. one parent, already-fetched, no DB change) before touching the descendant/multi-parent cases surfaces any wiring mistake on the cheapest possible surface area.

Output: `projectMarkers()` resolves GREEN/YELLOW/GREY correctly for root, descendant, and multi-parent cases; `SovereigntyAnalysis.comparedCapabilityIds` is a new, additive-only field; zero GraphQL schema, resolver, or client changes.
</objective>
